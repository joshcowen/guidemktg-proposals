HighLevel Communications Setup

The full build-out of the HighLevel subaccount, contact import, and all lifecycle automation sequences for the Society of Wetland Scientists Professional Certification Program.

The Scope

14 automation sequences. One blank subaccount. A lot of conditional logic.

Spartan Marketing is rebuilding SWSPCP's entire certification operations platform on WordPress. HighLevel is the communications layer. Every triggered email in the system runs through it. That includes application confirmations, reference requests, renewal reminders, approval and decline notices, account activations, and more.

The subaccount will be set up fresh under Spartan's HL account. Guide MKTG builds and tests everything inside it: all sequences, contact segmentation, conditional logic, and stop triggers. Spartan provides email copy and the final SWSPCP contact list. We do the build. See the full automation map →

This is not a simple drip campaign. Several of these sequences have meaningful conditional logic: the renewal reminder series has to stop the moment a renewal is submitted, reference notifications have to fire per individual reference as they come in, and the account activation sends need to go out in batches to roughly 3,500 contacts rather than all at once. That's the work.

What Gets Built

Every automated touchpoint in the certification lifecycle.

The sequences below map directly to the applicant, reviewer, and certificate holder workflows described in the Spartan proposal. Email copy for each touchpoint is provided by Spartan/SWSPCP. Our job is the configuration, logic, and testing.

Sequence 1

Account Creation Confirmation

Triggered on new account registration. Confirms the account and sets expectations before the applicant begins their application.

Sequence 2

Abandoned Application Reminder Series

Multi-step reminder series for applicants who start but don't submit. Stops automatically the moment the application is submitted. Eliminates the lost-application problem the current site has.

Sequence 3

Application Submitted Confirmation

Fires on submission. Confirms receipt and sets realistic expectations on timeline (24-48 hours for initial decision).

Sequence 4

Reference Request (Form C)

Sends the Form C link to each of 3 references individually when required. Tracks which references have responded and which are still outstanding.

Sequence 5

Reference Received Notification

Notifies the applicant each time an individual reference submits their Form C, so they can track progress without contacting staff.

Sequence 6

All References Complete Notification

Fires when the third reference submits. Tells the applicant the final review window (24-72 hours) so expectations are set before staff have to answer questions.

Sequence 7

Application Approved

Approval notification with instructions to access the certificate holder portal and download the certificate. Triggers for all certification paths.

Sequence 8

Application Declined

Decline notification with the reviewer's reason pulled in dynamically. Includes next steps for reapplication. No reason, no send.

Sequence 9

Incomplete Application Kickback

Notifies the applicant when a reviewer marks the application incomplete, includes the reviewer's notes on what is missing, and prompts them to correct and resubmit.

Sequence 10

Renewal Reminder Series (90 / 60 / 30 Days)

Three-touch renewal reminder series. The stop trigger is the critical piece here: the moment a renewal application is submitted, all pending reminders in the sequence cancel. No nagging people who already renewed.

Sequence 11

Outside Credit (CEU) Decision Notice

Notifies the certificate holder when an outside credit submission is approved or declined, with the decision detail pulled in dynamically.

Sequence 12

Annual Maintenance Fee Reminder

Annual reminder to certificate holders that the maintenance fee is due, with a direct link to the payment screen.

Sequence 13

Short Course Provider Submission / Approval

Submission confirmation for third-party providers and approval or decline notification once the committee has reviewed.

Sequence 14

Phased Account Activation (3,500 Contacts)

Account setup emails sent to the existing certificate holder database in batches, not all at once. Prevents a deliverability spike and gives staff time to handle support questions in waves.

Also included: Subaccount configuration, contact import and segmentation from the SWSPCP CSV, and full QA of every sequence end-to-end before handoff to Spartan.
Pre-Launch Requirement
Email Deliverability Setup

Before any sequence fires, the sending domain has to be ready. This means configuring SPF, DKIM, and DMARC DNS records for the SWSPCP sending subdomain in HL, then running a warming schedule (starting with low send volume and ramping up over 2-3 weeks) before the Sequence 14 activation blast goes to 3,500 contacts. It doesn't matter how engaged that list is. A brand-new sending domain has zero reputation with Gmail, Outlook, and the rest. Skip warming and the activation emails land in spam on day one.
