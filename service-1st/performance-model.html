What Counts as Your Cut
✓ Josh Gets Paid
1

Quote marked "Sent" in BuildOps

Estimator sends the quote as normal. Nothing changes on their end.

2

Passes all filters in Make.com

Not a portal job, not a PM contract, not an excluded customer.

3

Contact enters HighLevel

Make creates the contact and starts the follow-up sequence.

4

At least one message sent

Email or SMS goes out to the customer. This is the key trigger.

5

Quote approved within 90 days

BuildOps marks the quote as Approved. Make logs it as attributed.

✓ Josh gets 5% of the invoice amount
✗ Josh Gets Nothing
✗

Quote filtered out

Portal job, PM contract, or excluded customer. Never enters HL.

✗

Quote closes before first message

Customer approves on Day 0 before automation fires. No message = no attribution.

✗

Quote goes past 90 days

Approval comes in after the 90-day window. Too far out to attribute.

✗

Quote never closes

Sequence runs all three touchpoints, no response, quote goes dark.

✗ Josh gets $0. No risk to Service 1st.
