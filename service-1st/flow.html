Step-by-Step Flow
1
BuildOps

Quote Marked "Sent"

An estimator creates a quote in BuildOps and marks it as Sent. That single status change is the trigger for everything that follows. Nothing else needs to happen on your team's end.

2
Make.com

Make.com Picks It Up

Make.com is watching BuildOps via the API. The moment a quote is marked "Sent," Make pulls the relevant data: customer name, contact info, quote number, job scope, division, and estimator details.

3
Make.com

Filter Check: Should This Contact Get Follow-Ups?

Before anything reaches HighLevel, Make runs three exclusion checks. If a contact fails any check, they are dropped from the sequence entirely and never enter HL.

Filter 1: Portal Contacts

ServiceChannel & ResQ

Restaurant and portal-managed work goes through their own approval channels. These contacts are excluded automatically based on source tag in BuildOps.

Filter 2: Excluded Customers

Named Account List

Specific customers (e.g., Irvine Co.) who should never receive automated outreach are maintained on an exclusion list. Matched by company name or contact ID.

Filter 3: Contract Types

PMs, Yearly Testings, etc.

Recurring maintenance contracts and scheduled work orders are filtered by job type field. These are expected work, not open bids, so they don't need follow-up.

✓ Passes all three filters? Contact moves to HighLevel.
4
HighLevel

Contact & Opportunity Created

Make pushes the contact into HighLevel. A new opportunity is created in the correct division pipeline: Plumbing, HVAC, or Electrical. Custom fields are populated with quote number, job scope, property name, and the estimator's name, email, and phone number.

If the customer already exists in HighLevel from a previous quote, the existing record is updated rather than duplicated.

5
HighLevel

3-Step Follow-Up Sequence Fires

The workflow starts immediately. Each message goes out from the estimator who wrote the quote, not from a generic company address.

Day 0 — Quote Day
✉

Confirmation Email

Confirms the quote was sent, who the estimator is, and how to reach them with questions.

💬

Confirmation Text

Short SMS letting the customer know the quote is in their inbox and who sent it.

Day 2 — Follow-Up
✉

Follow-Up Email

Checks in, answers common questions, nudges toward a decision. Still from the estimator.

💬

Follow-Up Text

Brief touchpoint referencing the quote and offering to answer any questions.

Day 4 — Final Touch
✉

Final Email

Last automated touchpoint. After this the sequence ends and the contact is flagged for a live call.

💬

Final Text

Final automated SMS. Contact moves to the daily call report after this fires.

6
HighLevel

Sequence Stops — Six Ways This Ends

The sequence stops the moment any of the following happens. No over-contacting, no messages sent after a decision is made.

Quote Approved

BuildOps sends an approval status update. Make catches it, HL stops the sequence immediately, and the estimator gets a notification.

Quote Rejected

Customer declines or BuildOps marks the quote lost. Sequence stops and the opportunity is moved to Closed Lost in the pipeline.

Customer Needs More Time

Customer replies saying they're working on approvals. Estimator clicks one button in HL to pause the sequence. Optionally handled automatically via AI reply detection.

Customer Has Questions

Customer replies to an email or text. The reply lands in HighLevel. The estimator gets notified and the sequence pauses while the conversation is active.

Day 4 Completed

All three touchpoints sent with no response. Automation ends. Contact is added to the daily call report for the team to follow up by phone.

Manual Stop

Any estimator or manager can stop the sequence for any contact at any time directly from the HighLevel pipeline view.

7
HighLevel

Real-Time Notifications

Two events trigger an immediate notification to the estimator.

Quote Approved

Estimator gets notified the moment BuildOps marks the quote approved. No more finding out days later.

Customer Replies

Any inbound email or SMS reply from the customer triggers a notification to the estimator so they can respond while the conversation is warm.

8
Make.com

Two Automated Reports, No Manual Work

Both reports are built in Make.com. Each runs on a schedule, queries HighLevel for the relevant data, formats it, and emails it automatically. No spreadsheet, no pulling data by hand, no logging into HighLevel to check.

Daily — Sent Each Morning

Day 4 Call List

Every morning, Make.com queries HighLevel for all contacts who completed their third follow-up in the past 24 hours. It formats those into a clean list and emails it to the right people. These are the ones who didn't respond and need a live call from your team that day.

  • Customer name and contact info
  • Quote number and job scope
  • Which estimator owns the quote
  • Division (Plumbing, HVAC, or Electrical)
Weekly — Sent Every Monday

Performance Summary

Every Monday, Make.com queries HighLevel for the previous week's pipeline activity across all three divisions, calculates the numbers, and emails a summary to management. Visibility into how the system is performing without logging in anywhere.

  • Quotes entered into the sequence
  • Approvals, rejections, and open quotes
  • Close rate by division
  • Reply rate and sequence completion rate
