What We Heard

$900 a month in Google Ads. No way to prove it's working.

Foreign Accents is running a real marketing operation. Google Ads, a Google Business Profile, monthly email campaigns, weekly specials. The activity is there. The problem is that none of it connects. Every decision about what to spend and where to spend it is being made on gut feel because there's no system tying a marketing dollar to an inbound call to a booked appointment.

Here's what that looks like in practice:

  • The Google Ads account shows clicks and impressions, but there's no way to trace a click to an actual call or confirm that call turned into a repair order. The data stops at the click.
  • New vs. returning callers are indistinguishable at the point of first contact. The shop answers every call cold, with no context on whether this person has been in before or what they're likely to want.
  • Call-to-appointment conversion rate is estimated, not measured. Nobody actually knows what percentage of inbound calls become booked appointments, and nobody knows why the ones that don't convert fell off.
  • Weekly specials are one of the shop's main marketing levers, but there's no way to tell which specials drive call volume and which ones go unnoticed. The specials calendar is built on instinct.
  • The Q2 goal is $125,000 per month, up from $100,000. That's 70 additional repair orders needed. It's nearly impossible to close a gap you can't measure.

Claude API sits inside the Make.com layer. Every call transcript gets sent to Claude automatically. It reads the transcript and returns structured data: was an appointment booked, what service was requested, did the caller mention a weekly special, what was the sentiment. That data lands in the same Google Sheet as everything else, no manual entry required.
1

Every Call Gets Tagged

CallRail assigns a unique tracking number to each marketing channel. When someone calls any of those numbers, CallRail logs the source, records the call, generates a transcript, and forwards it to the shop. Nothing changes for the staff.

2

AI Reads Every Transcript

Make.com sends each transcript to the Claude API, which returns structured data on what happened: appointment booked or not, service requested, weekly special mentioned, caller sentiment, whether follow-up is needed. Consistent, queryable, automatic.

3

The Dashboard Shows Everything

All data flows into Google Sheets and up into a Looker Studio dashboard. Call volume by channel, cost per call, conversion rates, specials performance, new vs. returning breakdown. Refreshes every four hours with no manual intervention.

CallRail call conversions imported back into Google Ads as conversion actions. The Ads account can now optimize toward actual calls, not just clicks. Cost per call by campaign becomes visible for the first time.

Two structured sheets: a calls log with all CallRail and Claude API fields, and a manual RO entry sheet where the shop logs daily repair orders and revenue. Together they tell the full story from call to closed ticket.

Make.com Automation Workflows

Two workflows built and tested: CallRail to Sheets on every completed call, and Sheets transcript to Claude API to Sheets with structured output. Both include error handling, retry logic, and a failure notification workflow.

Looker Studio Dashboard (5 Modules)

Volume and Revenue Overview, Lead Source Attribution, New vs. Returning Customer Breakdown, Call Performance and Conversion Analysis, Weekly Specials Performance. Shareable via link with no login required for stakeholders.

SOPs + Handoff Documentation

Written documentation covering how the system works, how to add or update a tracking number, what to do when a Make scenario errors, and how to read the dashboard. Spartan owns it clean from day one.

A note on Steer and the phone system

The original scope doc included pulling repair order data directly from Steer via API and using Steer's Call Intelligence layer for outcome tracking. After digging in, Steer doesn't appear to offer a public REST API, and the shop's Yealink phone system isn't compatible with Steer Call Intelligence (which requires RingCentral or native integration). The revenue side of the dashboard is covered by a simple manual entry sheet where the shop logs daily ROs and revenue. It takes about two minutes a day and keeps the data clean. If Steer opens up an API down the road, connecting it is a straightforward Phase 3 add-on.
